Corporate Volunteer Opportunities

Bring Your Team Together for the Magic of Christmas

Bring Your Team Together for the Magic of Christmas

The Magic of Christmas welcomes corporate teams to join us at our warehouse and help bring the spirit of Christmas to families in our community.

Volunteering together is a meaningful way to give back, build connections as a team, and make a real difference during the holiday season.

How Does Corporate Volunteering Work?

From November 12 to December 22, we welcome volunteer groups to our warehouse every day of the week.

  • Group size: Typically 10–20 people
  • Volunteer shifts: Approximately 2–3 hours
  • Availability: Shifts are available every day, subject to availability
  • Location: Santa’s Workshop – #32 – 4216 54 AVE SE
  • Cost: There is no cost, but volunteers are encouraged to bring gift cards or donations (View our wish list here)

Your team may help with a variety of activities depending on what we need that day. Tasks can include:

  • Sorting and organizing donations
  • Packing gifts for families
  • Preparing food hampers
  • Setting up for events
  • Organizing and preparing items throughout the warehouse
  • Other seasonal tasks that help keep The Magic of Christmas running smoothly

Every group plays an important role in helping us prepare for the holiday season!

Interested in Bringing Your Team?

We’d love to have your company join us!

Please complete the Corporate Volunteer Request Form below to tell us a little about your group and your preferred dates and times.

If we have availability that works for your team, one of our volunteers will be in touch with information about our booking calendar and next steps.

Looking for Other Ways to Get Your Team Involved?

Volunteering is just one way your company can support The Magic of Christmas. Consider these additional opportunities:

Company Donation Matching

Many companies will match charitable donations made by their employees, helping your team’s contributions go even further. Check with your employer to see if a donation-matching program is available.

Benevity

Does your company use Benevity? You may be able to turn your volunteer hours into additional support for The Magic of Christmas. Some companies provide charitable grants based on the number of hours their employees volunteer.

Host a Santa Party

Bring the magic of Christmas to your workplace by hosting a Santa Party for your team or community.

Whether your team volunteers at our warehouse, matches employee donations, participates through Benevity, hosts a Santa Party, or becomes a sponsor, your support helps make the holiday season brighter for families in our community.
