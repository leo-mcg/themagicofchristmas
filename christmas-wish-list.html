Christmas Wish List

The Magic of Christmas visits Calgarians of all ages, from newborns to grandparents. Check out our Christmas Wish List below by group or by categories.

Want to donate gifts, but unsure of where to start? The Magic of Christmas visits Calgarians of all ages, from newborns to grandparents. Check out our Christmas Wish List below by group or by categories.

Please note that all gift donations that arrive at Santa’s workshop must be new, unused and unwrapped. We cannot accept used or worn donations.

The motto at the Magic of Christmas is; Loving, Caring, and Sharing so we ask that toys and gifts donated promote this message, please do not donate toys or gifts that promote violence, such as guns, violent or scary movies as examples.
