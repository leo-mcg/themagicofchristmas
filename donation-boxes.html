Request a donation box for your organization

We invite Calgary’s working force to donate gifts from the comfort of their workspaces. Simply request one or more donation boxes for your organization anytime between November and Christmas Eve. Besides knowing that your gifts will go toward a family in need, this can make for a fun and meaningful initiative for you and your colleagues.

(Please note this is not a donation pick up service for individual households. Business addresses only as personal home addresses will be showing up on our public map)

Please submit a request for a box here or call Santa’s workshop at 403-803-1619.

For business owners/organization coordinators who want to print off more posters, download a digital copy to display around your office.
