Message From Our Community

It is a constant reminder that The Magic of Christmas is the sum of the community.

This year, we’ve partnered with Canada Helps as our fundraising solution provider. When a donation is made, the donor has an option to leave a message to us. The sheer volume of love and support we’ve been getting since November has been very moving to say the least. It is a constant reminder that The Magic of Christmas is the sum of the community. We would like to share a few of them to everyone.

“Loving, Caring, and Sharing.”

The Magic of Christmas is a 100% volunteer, non-denominational charity based in the city of Calgary.
