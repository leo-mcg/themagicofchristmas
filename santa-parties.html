Christmas Party with Santa

Our passionate Party Santa’s and Party Elves attend community and corporate Christmas parties throughout November and December.

Christmas Party with Santa and his elves is back!!! Our passionate Party Santa’s and Party Elves attend various community and cooperate Christmas parties throughout November and December.

What is a Santa Party?

Do you need a Santa for an upcoming Christmas Party? The Magic of Christmas can provide Santas and Elves to attend your party. When you hire a Santa and elves from us, all the money goes to support The Magic of Christmas and it helps us bring cheer to more families each year.

Things Santa and his Elves can do:

  • Take pictures
  • Sing Songs
  • Mingle throughout your party
  • Hand out treats (*Not provided by us)
  • Something else in mind? Ask us!

Suggested minimum honorarium for different party sizes.

All proceeds go directly to The Magic of Christmas

Small Party

  • Santa and 1-2 Elves
  • Up to 1 hour
  • Suggested Minimum Honorarium – $350

Medium Party

  • Santa and 2-3 Elves
  • 1-2 Hours
  • Suggested Minimum Honorarium – $500

Large Party

  • Santa and 3+ Elves
  • 2-3 Hours
  • Suggested Minimum Honorarium – $750

If you are looking to rent-a-Santa for your upcoming Christmas party this holiday season and want to support a great cause, fill out the following request form. Our elves will contact you to confirm the booking.
