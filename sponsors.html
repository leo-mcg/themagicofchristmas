Sponsors

The Magic of Christmas appreciates our sponsors and donors who have chosen to support our charity in our city.

Their support allows us to move mountains by donating significantly, may it be through finances, gifts, nonperishable food, time, charitable events or publicity. Not a single donation goes unnoticed.
