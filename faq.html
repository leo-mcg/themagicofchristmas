Registration Process

Why do we fill out a volunteer registration form?

A: Each year, we ask all volunteers — whether you’re joining us for the first time or returning for another season — to complete the registration form. This helps us meet our insurance requirements and keep our records up to date. It also ensures we can organize shifts and assign volunteer roles efficiently, so everyone is placed where they can best help spread the Magic of Christmas!

What happens after I submit my registration form online?

A: Your name and information will be automatically input into our system.

We thank you for your patience as we organize the hundreds of volunteer forms we receive each year. When we’re ready, our Volunteer Coordinator will reach out to you to confirm your volunteer schedule.

We have a group that wants to volunteer on Christmas Eve on a bus, how do you know we are all together?

A: GROUP NAME!! Fill out a volunteer registration for EVERYONE in the group, and ensure that you all have the same group name.

I didn’t get a confirmation email after I filled out a volunteer registration form?

A: Check your junk and spam folders. If not there, fill it out again – ensure that you have filled out every aspect, AND that you do not use the back button when you are finished with your form, as this will create an error.

Ongoing Volunteer Opportunities

When do you need volunteers the most?

A: Our need for volunteers increases in December, especially in the few weeks leading up to Christmas. Please keep checking back the volunteer opportunities page for updates on the support we need: https://www.themagicofchristmas.org/volunteer-opps/.

I don’t see any available volunteer opportunities on the website, when will they be available?

A: We post them on an as needed basis, so keep checking back.

I want to get more involved in committees. How do I do that?

A: Fill out the volunteer registration, and select the committee you want, if we need help in that area, we will reach out to you.

I want to help pack bags in the warehouse, how can I sign up for those shifts?

A: They will be available on the volunteer opportunities page. We post them on an as needed basis, so keep checking back throughout the season.

Corporate/Team Groups

I want to bring a group of 10+ people to the warehouse. Is this possible?

A: Yes! We bring corporate groups and teams into our warehouse. However, we have limited spaces available. Email volunteer@themagicofchristmas.org with your group and how many people, then we can figure out if there is a date available.

Christmas Eve Buses

How long are bus shifts?

A: All bus shifts are 6-8 hours long, depending on many factors. Buses leave the warehouse every 30 minutes from 7am-5pm.

I can only do a morning shift on a bus – Does that work?

A: We have limited spaces available for morning slots on Christmas Eve, and it is our most popular requested shift, a bus shift is not guaranteed, but we are happy to TRY to accommodate any request we can.

I registered to be a bus elf, what do I wear?

A: We provide all elf costumes! We have a fabulous team who has hand makes elf costumes for many years. This is a MoC tradition. We request that you wear black or white, leggings/long johns with a black or white long sleeve shirt. Please refrain from wearing clothes with lots of logos/images.

When Will I know the shifts that I’ve been scheduled for?

A: For buses: We aim to send out all bus assignments in the last week of November. All other assignments will be sent out as soon as they are ready, we try our best to have it be 14+ days before your shift.

What if I don’t have enough people to fill a bus?

A: THAT IS OK!!!!!!! We combine other groups to create a full bus.

How many people are on each bus?

A: This varies on a case-by-case basis, but normally, 12-16 people is where we try to keep the buses.

What time do buses on Christmas Eve depart?

A: We have buses leaving the warehouse every 30 minutes from 7am-5pm.

When will bus assignments go out?

A: Expect bus assignments to be sent out the last week of November.

I signed up to be on a bus, but I didn’t get assigned, why?

A: We have over 1000 people request to be on a bus – and around 700 spaces for elves. We just don’t have enough space for everyone.

We need to be done on xmas eve by a certain time, when will our shift be over?

A: We estimate that shifts are 6-8 hours long. But we cannot guarantee anything – there are so many factors, how many visits are assigned to your bus, weather, distance from warehouse, if you were on time departing the warehouse etc. Therefore, we cannot guarantee when your shift will finish.

Do I have to wear a costume?

A: All elves and Santas are required to wear our costumes as provided. Our elf costumes consist of a single jumper-style outfit, or knickers and a vest. You will need to provide a solid white or black turtleneck, leggings/tights and appropriate shoes to complete your elfish ensemble. Bare legs, printed slogans on shirts, etc. will not be permitted.

Do I have to sing carols while I'm out volunteering?

A: ALL of Santa’s elves must sing, no matter what they think they sound like. Carol singing is an essential part of The Magic of Christmas. You are expected to sing and know the words to popular songs, including Rudolph, Jingle Bells and Here Comes Santa Claus. Download The Magic of Christmas songbook here.

Hospital/Care Centre Visits

When are the hospital and care centre visits?

A: This is a case-by-case basis – there are many times we get enough hospital registrants that are only available for hospital visits, therefore, there are many times where you go on one and not both.

Can I be on both a hospital visit and a bus?

A: December 23rd

General

I don’t want to go on a bus, are there other things I can do on Christmas Eve?

A: We need hotshot drivers, bus loaders, costume team (no sewing) – read volunteer position page for more details: https://www.themagicofchristmas.org/volunteer/

What is Elf Rally? Do I need to go?

A: If you have received a confirmation to be on a bus, we encourage you to come to Elf Rally, as this is when we show how a bus shift will happen.

I don’t think I am receiving MoC emails, what can I do?

A: Add volunteer@themagicofchristmas.org to your contacts/email list to ensure that our emails are going through.

How will I know when I am needed?

A: Our volunteer coordinator will contact you directly by email. Please ensure you have added volunteer@themagicofchristmas.org into your contacts to not miss any emails.

Do I have to volunteer on Christmas Eve? What if I only want to help in the warehouse?

A: Individuals wishing to help out only in the warehouse can sign up through our volunteer registration as a ‘warehouse elf’ – or can watch our volunteer opportunities page for warehouse opportunities https://www.themagicofchristmas.org/volunteer-opps/
