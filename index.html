Helping others believe again in the Magic of Christmas

Our mission is to deliver Christmas spirit into the homes and hearts of those who need it most and invite everyone to believe (again!) in the true spirit and The Magic of Christmas.

Every Christmas Eve

Christmas magic comes but once a year.

Have you spotted the jolly, bearded man in red delivering joy to those in need?

The Magic of Christmas is a 100% volunteer, non-denominational charity based in the city of Calgary.

Every Christmas Eve, we load up Calgary Transit buses, magically transformed into “reindeer buses,” with hundreds of gifts and volunteers dressed as Santa and his elves. Then, we do what we do best—deliver Christmas spirit into the homes and hearts of those who need it most.

It’s magic you’ll want to be a part of.
